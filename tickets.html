Questions

Frequently asked

Can I buy tickets at the door?

Only if there are tickets left. The exhibition is open for one day only and places are limited, so we recommend booking online in advance. Any remaining tickets are sold at the entrance on 13 November.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No. Show the e-ticket on your phone at the entrance.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Because the exhibition takes place on a single day, tickets cannot be refunded or moved to another date. Can’t make it? You can give your ticket to someone else — the e-ticket is not linked to a name.

Who qualifies for a reduced ticket?

Students, visitors aged 65 and over, and visitors with a disability. Please bring a valid student card or ID, as it may be checked at the entrance. Children under 12 have their own ticket.
