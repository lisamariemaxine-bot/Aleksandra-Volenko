The artist

Aleksandra Volenko

1899 — ?

Aleksandra Volenko was a Russian ballerina, trained at the Imperial Ballet School in Saint Petersburg. In 1917 she fled the revolution to Denmark, drawing the journey along the way.

Her expressive, almost otherworldly performances made her name, until the war closed the theatres. In 1948 she disappeared from the ballet world. What remains are fragments: photographs, drawings and a diary.
